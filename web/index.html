This phone is the sensor

Ready when you are

Motion access is granted per page and only from a tap — iOS will not hand over the sensors any other way. Nothing is read before you tap.

While streaming, this phone sends heading, tilt and acceleration magnitude — never your location — to the Worker you paired with. Readings sit in a buffer of at most 30 minutes that erases itself; they are processed only on the basis of your consent. Tapping Start is that consent, and “End & erase” withdraws it.
